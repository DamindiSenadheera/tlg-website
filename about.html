ABOUT TL GROUPS

A company built to keep
good software moving.

Five companies, two studios, and one long-term bet: useful software should not disappear because its owners ran out of time, focus, or appetite.

THE BET

Good products do not need a new name. They need room to work again.

Most acquisitions optimise for consolidation. We optimise for continuity: the product remains recognisable to its customers, while the company gains the systems and focus it needs to improve.

It started in 2015 above a bookshop in Lisbon, when our three founders — two engineers and one accountant who'd grown tired of watching good products get strangled by their own growth — pooled a small amount of savings and bought a struggling invoicing tool nobody else wanted. They fixed the parts that were broken, listened to the customers nobody had listened to in years, and within eighteen months it was profitable again for the first time since 2013.

That company is still with us. It's called Ledgerly, and it's still run by the same product lead we promoted in year two. Since then, the model hasn't changed much. We look for products with a loyal, underserved customer base and a team that's stopped being given the resources to do their best work. We acquire the whole company — not just the product — move its infrastructure onto Compass, our shared operating platform, and then get out of the way. No headquarters relocations, no forced rebrands, no quarterly targets set by someone who has never used the product.

A longer horizon for useful software.

Our vision

To help the software people depend on remain useful, trusted, and recognisable—long after a change in ownership might otherwise have ended its story.

Our mission

TL Groups gives proven software companies a permanent home, shared operating systems, and the patient focus to improve what customers already value.

The standards we keep.

Reliability first

Uptime and trust are not features; they are the baseline. We build systems and habits that work consistently.

Reliability is a promise

When customers depend on a product, dependable service is a commitment—not a number reserved for a presentation.

Ownership

Every team member treats the problem in front of them as their own—not someone else’s ticket.

Speed with discipline

We move quickly when it matters, without trading away quality, accountability, or customer trust.

Transparency

We choose clear communication with customers, collaborators, and each other over politics or ambiguity.

Continuous improvement

Products, systems, processes, and people keep evolving. Good enough is never the final destination.

Ethical stewardship

We take responsibility for the companies, customers, and teams placed in our care—and make decisions we can stand behind for the long term.

What we are working toward.

  1. Keep the company recognisable.Customers should still know the product they chose and the people behind it.
  2. Strengthen what sits beneath the surface.Shared systems should make each crew more capable without making every company the same.
  3. Let customer value compound.We measure progress in dependable improvements that last, not a short holding period.

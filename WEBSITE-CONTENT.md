# About

## Hero

- **Eyebrow:** ABOUT TL GROUPS
- **Headline:** The current beneath the *islands*.
- **Intro:** TL Groups finds software companies that did something genuinely well, then lost momentum — under-resourced, over-managed, or simply tired. We buy them outright, fold them onto shared infrastructure, and hand them back their focus.

## Story

It started in 2015 above a bookshop in Lisbon, when our three founders — two engineers and one accountant who'd grown tired of watching good products get strangled by their own growth — pooled a small amount of savings and bought a struggling invoicing tool nobody else wanted. They fixed the parts that were broken, listened to the customers nobody had listened to in years, and within eighteen months it was profitable again for the first time since 2013.

> That company is still with us. It's called Ledgerly, and it's still run by the same product lead we promoted in year two.

Since then, the model hasn't changed much. We look for products with a loyal, underserved customer base and a team that's stopped being given the resources to do their best work. We acquire the whole company — not just the product — move its infrastructure onto Compass, our shared operating platform, and then get out of the way. No headquarters relocations, no forced rebrands, no quarterly targets set by someone who has never used the product.

We've never sold an island. Decisions get made for the ten-year version of the company, not the next board meeting.
